Chemistry · Rates of reaction

Reaction Rates.

Some reactions are over in a flash; others take years. Particles have to collide, and collide hard enough, before they can react. Change how often and how hard they collide, and you change how fast the reaction goes.

Key words

Rate of reactionHow fast reactants are used up, or products are made, e.g. how much gas is given off each second
Collision theoryParticles must collide to react, and the collision must have enough energy. More successful collisions each second means a faster reaction
Activation energyThe smallest amount of energy a collision needs for the particles to react. Gentler collisions just bounce apart
ConcentrationHow many particles are packed into a given volume. Higher concentration, more frequent collisions
Surface areaHow much of a solid is exposed. Only surface particles can be hit, so a powder reacts faster than a lump
CatalystA substance that speeds up a reaction by giving it a route that needs less energy. It is not used up
